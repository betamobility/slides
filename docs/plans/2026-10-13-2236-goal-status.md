### Three rounds failed on the same step: what was assumed, what the evidence says, what changes

**Assumed:** that remembering a pairing in flight (so a command killed by the agent's shell does not waste the person's click) was a small addition that could be hardened one finding at a time.

**The evidence:** round 1's findings were against U6 and U7 as planned, and their fixes held. Every P0 and P1 in rounds 2 and 3 was inside the resume mechanism, which the plan did not ask for; the code review offered it as one of two answers to its P1, the other being a skill instruction, and both were built. The same two faults came back in new shapes each round: a URL read back from the pending file and printed, and two runs polling one handle.

**What changes:** `a4665d58` removes both faults by design instead of by validation: no URL is stored or read back (the link is rebuilt from the store address and a ten-character code), and one run at a time collects, under a lock, while any other run waits for the saved grant. **The fork for round 4 is decided in advance:** if round 4 reports a P0 or P1 in the pending or lock code, the mechanism is removed, not patched a fourth time, and the skill instruction (run the pairing in the background, the click is the person's) stands alone as the answer to the review's P1. Everything round 1 fixed is independent of it. If round 4 is clean, the design stays and round 5 runs. A P0 or P1 in round 5 or 6 is the cap: the unit stops here with the reason, no seventh attempt.
