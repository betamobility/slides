### Two rounds found holes in the still's transport: what was assumed, what the evidence says, what changes

I assumed a library still travels with its slide because U8's comment says its 64 KB budget makes it "travel inline with the first frame", and that the encoding was the only thing to get right. The evidence says no encoding can keep that promise. The inline limit is 64 K characters of data URI. Base64 grows any still by a third, and percent-encoding grows CJK text ninefold. ### Two rounds found holes in the YAML header pattern: what was assumed, what the evidence says, what changes

I assumed a single-line pattern could recognise every block-scalar header. YAML says otherwise: a header can carry tags and anchors, either order of the indentation and chomping indicators, and a trailing comment, and each round found one more form. What changes: the rig no longer reads headers. A `#` line counts as a marker only when none of its ancestors (each earlier line with a smaller indent, up to column 0) has `|` or `>` in its value. A block scalar's text is always indented under the line that opens it, whatever the header spells. The rule fails closed: an odd layout can make CI refuse a real marker, but it can never accept a forgotten one. A generated sweep checks 252 header and depth layouts both ways, and with the rule switched off the sweep fails. No YAML parser is added: none is installed, and `slides/package.json` conflicts at every upstream merge.
